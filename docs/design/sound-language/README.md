# Operator sound language

One instrument (soft sine/triangle), one key (A), silence by default.
Pitch direction carries meaning: rising = leaving you, falling = arriving to you,
flat = a fact with no direction, low = stopping.

Never sounds: typing, scrolling, app open, thinking, reads.

| # | Moment | Sound | Shape | Why | Listen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You send a request | Send | two quick notes rising A5→E6, 120 ms, light haptic | Your words leaving. Rising = away from you. | [▶ 1-send.wav](sounds/1-send.wav) |
| 2 | Operator changes something in an app (write only) | Did | one soft note with its octave below, 500 ms slow fade, pitched per app within the key | The "watch it happen" sound. Per-app pitch teaches which app without looking. | [▶ calendar](sounds/2-did-calendar.wav) · [gmail](sounds/2-did-gmail.wav) · [maps](sounds/2-did-maps.wav) · [drive](sounds/2-did-drive.wav) |
| 3 | Turn finishes, answer lands | Done | two notes falling E6→A5, 300 ms | Mirror of Send: arriving to you. | [▶ 3-done.wav](sounds/3-done.wav) |
| 4 | Operator stops and needs you (sheet rises) | Needs you | low held A3 with a soft fifth, 500 ms, firm haptic | The only interrupting sound. Low and long so it is unmistakable. Never repeats; the pulsing logo carries it after. | [▶ 4-needs-you.wav](sounds/4-needs-you.wav) |
| 5 | You confirm on the sheet | Committed | Did note then Done fall, 540 ms | Two known sounds chained; no new vocabulary for a rare moment. | [▶ 5-committed.wav](sounds/5-committed.wav) |
| 6 | You decline, or press Stop | Left it | two low notes stepping down A3→E3, 620 ms | Closing a door quietly. Low like Needs-you, short because nothing is demanded. | [▶ 6-left-it.wav](sounds/6-left-it.wav) |
| 7 | Failure or unknown outcome | Unsure | two flat notes D5 D5, 420 ms, slightly detuned | No direction, no progress. Off, not alarming. Never a buzzer. | [▶ 7-unsure.wav](sounds/7-unsure.wav) |
| 8 | Someone else's message arrives that Operator offers to answer | Incoming | one falling note E5→A4, 200 ms | Half of Done: arrived, nothing finished. Quieter than Done. | [▶ 8-incoming.wav](sounds/8-incoming.wav) |

Shared DNA
- Send / Done are mirrors (rise vs fall): the round trip.
- Needs-you / Left-it / Committed sit around A3: the stopping register.
- Did is the only per-app sound; everything else is Operator's own voice.

Volume ladder (relative)
Needs-you 100 · Done 70 · Committed 70 · Send 60 · Left-it 60 · Incoming 50 · Unsure 50 · Did 45.
Did and Left-it were shorter and quieter at first; too easy to miss on a phone speaker, so lengthened (2026-09-18).

Settings
- "Sounds" on/off.
- "Only when it needs me": keeps #4 and #7 only.
- Follows the iPhone silent switch; Needs-you still haptics when silent.

Listen: open `listen.html` (play buttons + the Goa story as sound only). WAVs are in `sounds/`, generated by `sounds/make-sounds.py`.

Incoming (#8) is forward-looking: Operator does not start conversations yet.
When it does, the arrival is an iOS push notification, and this sound ships as
that notification's custom sound (a bundled audio file named in the push), so it
plays even when the app is closed. In-app it plays the same file.

Status (2026-09-18): all 8 agreed by ear. The v3 mock plays all of them:
Send, Did (writes only, per-app pitch), Done, Needs-you on the sheet, Committed on
yes, Left-it on no and on Stop, Unsure at the end of the Drive story, Incoming at
the start of the Maya story. Not yet in the iOS app.

Where things live
- This folder is the committed copy. Working copies are in the design folder
  (`Operator sound language.md`, `Operator sounds.html`, `sounds/`).
- Playable page: https://claude.ai/artifact/YTQMEz4nWdo8QAFEFy76jV
- v3 mock that plays the full set: https://claude.ai/artifact/GKzwU1UcooDBYcZ4kQRaeM
- Regenerate the WAVs: `python3 sounds/make-sounds.py sounds`. Every WAV is
  normalised to the same peak, so loudness between sounds is set only by the
  volume ladder at playback, not by the `gain` values in the script.
