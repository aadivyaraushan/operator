Operator sounds

One instrument, one key (A), silence by default. Pitch direction carries meaning: rising leaves you, falling arrives to you, flat is a fact, low is a stop. Tap a circle to hear it.

1 · Send
You send a request
Two quick notes rising, A5 → E6, 120 ms. Light haptic tap.
Your words leaving your hands. Rising = away from you.
2 · Did
Operator changed something in an app (held a date, saved a draft, moved files). Writes only; reads stay silent.
One soft note with its octave below, 500 ms with a slow fade, pitched per app within the key.
The "watch it happen" sound. Per-app pitch teaches you which app without looking. Quiet because it fires on every write; long enough to register on a phone speaker.
3 · Done
The turn finishes and the answer lands
Two notes falling, E6 → A5, 300 ms.
Mirror of Send: something arriving to you. This is the Cursor "message" moment.
4 · Needs you
Operator stops and needs you; the sheet rises
One low held note, A3, with a soft fifth on top, 500 ms. Firm haptic.
The only sound allowed to interrupt. Low and long so it is unmistakably different from the quick high ones. Never repeats; the pulsing logo carries it after.
5 · Committed
You confirm on the sheet (Book it, Send, Pay)
Did note, then Done fall, 540 ms.
Two known sounds chained: it acted, then it is back to you. No new vocabulary for a rare moment.
6 · Left it
You decline on the sheet, or press Stop
Two low notes stepping down, A3 → E3, 620 ms. Same shape as Done, two octaves lower and slower.
Closing a door quietly. Low like Needs-you (same family: stopping), short because nothing is demanded.
7 · Unsure
Something failed, or the outcome is unknown (connection dropped mid-batch)
Two identical flat notes, D5 D5, 420 ms, second slightly detuned.
Flat = no direction, no progress. The detune feels "off" without being an alarm. Never a buzzer; Operator is not in trouble, it is telling you the truth.
8 · Incoming
A message from someone else arrives that Operator offers to reply to
One falling note, E5 → A4, 200 ms.
Half of Done: something arrived, nothing finished. Quieter than Done.
